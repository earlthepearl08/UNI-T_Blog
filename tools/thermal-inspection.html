ΔT Triage

Classify a hotspot against the NETA temperature-rise table. Compare against a similar component under similar load where you can — it is the far more sensitive test.

Similar-component comparison catches faults far earlier: 4 °C is already a probable deficiency, where against ambient you would need 11 °C.
The matching phase or terminal, or the ambient air.
NETA guidance is to survey at 40% minimum, ideally at the highest normal load.

Load Correction

Resistive heating rises with the square of current. A fault surveyed at part load looks far milder than it will be at full load — this is the single most common way a thermal survey understates a fault.
